How to play

  • In Blitz, both players start with a charged special. One 25-second round decides the match. A tie replays the round.
  • In local versus, P1 uses the left pad and P2 uses the right. In solo practice, the CPU takes the other pad.
  • Move and jump to change range. Light attacks are quick. Heavy attacks hit harder.
  • Land a heavy hit with 60 energy, then tap Super while its button says Chain to skip the heavy recovery.
  • Hold Guard to block. At 60 energy, tap Super for your special move.
  • Guard just before impact to earn a perfect block. Release Guard and land a light or heavy attack for a stronger counter.
  • In Best of 3, each round lasts 45 seconds. A knockout or higher health at time-up wins the round. First to two rounds wins.
  • Keyboard: 1P uses A/D, W, S and J/K/L. 2P uses the arrows and comma, period and slash.
