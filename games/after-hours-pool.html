Call your pocket

One call per player. Sink a ball in your called pocket for 3 points. Other pockets score 1.

Match rules

  • Tap or drag to aim. The blue line shows the cue ball’s bank path.
  • Each player gets six shots. Score 1 per object ball, or 2 for sinking it in the gold pocket.
  • Once per player, Call a pocket assigns the 3-point pocket for one shot. A scratch cancels all points for that shot. The next player places the cue ball in the highlighted area.
  • The match ends after 12 shots or when the table clears. High score wins.
  • You get three attempts per trick shot. The layout resets between attempts.
  • Pocket the marked ball in the glowing pocket without scratching.
  • On the last table, bank the cue ball off the right rail before pocketing the marked ball.
