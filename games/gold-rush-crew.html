Drop Tap when the dotted line meets your target. The circle predicts moving cargo. Tap again during a cast to retract early.
Diamonds Each shaft has a violet diamond worth 300 points.
Diamond Hunt In Diamond Hunt, bank one violet diamond per shaft. These count separately from the blue mission crystals.
Drop stone If a hook brings back a stone, tap it again to drop the load. Give up 20 points to reel in faster.
Combo Rush Build combos with both hooks. Once one hook lands a haul worth at least 90 points, bank another with the other hook within 2.4 seconds.
Bomb Hook a bomb to start a 2.6 s fuse. The blast cracks rock, destroys loose ore in the circle and costs 5 seconds. Nearby bombs can chain.
Obstacles Cracked rock blocks a hook. Wait for rolling boulders to clear your path. Carts, crystals and diamonds resist blasts.
Brake and boost Brake stops both empty hooks for 2.6 s. Boost speeds up the slowest laden hook.
